Tropical Cyclone 24-hour Forecast

Deep-learning ensemble using storm history, geography, sea-surface temperature, vertical wind shear and steering flow. Runs entirely in your browser.

Research / education demo. Not an operational forecast; never use it for safety decisions. Follow your national weather service (IMD in India).
Map:

Pick a storm (all were held out from training) and a forecast time, then compare the forecast with what actually happened.

The default map draws coastlines and countries with no key or tile server. If the background does not load (offline), choose Plain in the Map menu at the top right: the tracks and circles still show.

Enter 9 or more consecutive 6-hourly observations, oldest first. Lat, lon, wind and distance to land are required; leave the optional columns empty to use typical values (real SST, shear and steering winds improve accuracy). Tip: you can paste rows copied from a spreadsheet into any cell.
